Walking Robot

Teach a bipedal robot to walk. Press W to walk forward, S to walk backward.

How to play: Press W to walk forward. S to reverse. P to push (test balance). Space to jump. Tune the sliders to find the perfect gait that goes the farthest without falling. The robot auto-balances using a PID controller — bad settings = faceplant.
